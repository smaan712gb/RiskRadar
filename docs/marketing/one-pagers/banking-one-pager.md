# RisksRadarAI for Banking & Credit Unions

## The Challenge

Your BSA/AML compliance program faces impossible odds:
- Transaction monitoring tools see overrides but not the employee who skipped AML training
- SIEM tools see after-hours access but not the wire transfers that followed
- HR tools see attendance anomalies but not the new payees created the same day
- **The compound pattern across these systems is where insider fraud hides**

Average insider threat cost: **$6.9M per incident** (IBM/Ponemon)

## The Solution

RisksRadarAI fuses signals across your core banking, SIEM, HRIS, and communication systems to detect compound risk patterns weeks before they become incidents.

### What You Get

**AML & Financial Crime Monitoring**
- Transaction override forensics with supervisor absence correlation
- Structuring/smurfing detection (just-below-threshold pattern analysis)
- Benford's Law analysis on transaction and expense amounts
- Auto SAR/STR draft generation in FinCEN format
- 30-day filing deadline tracking with automated reminders

**Cross-Domain Insider Threat Detection**
- Signals correlated across 5 domains simultaneously
- Evidence briefs with chain-of-thought AI reasoning
- MITRE ATT&CK kill chain staging
- Predictive risk trajectories with projected breach dates

**Autonomous Regulatory Compliance**
- Scans FinCEN, OCC, FDIC, SEC, OFAC every 6 hours
- Auto-proposes policy updates for CIO/CCO approval
- Tests and deploys approved changes automatically
- 25 regulatory rules covering BSA/AML, SOX, GLBA

**Built for BSA Examinations**
- Immutable audit trail (7-year retention, DB-enforced)
- Every AI decision explainable with source citations
- Read-only regulator portal for OCC/FDIC examiners
- Complete evidence export (JSON/PDF)
