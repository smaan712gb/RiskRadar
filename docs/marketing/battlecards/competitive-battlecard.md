## Common Objections & Responses

### "We already have [competitor tool]"
*"That's great — they handle [their domain] well. But do they correlate that with your HR data, communication patterns, and financial transactions? The compound pattern across domains is what we uniquely detect. We complement your existing tools."*

### "Open source means insecure / immature"
*"Apache 2.0 is the same license as Kubernetes, TensorFlow, and Android. Being open source means your security team can verify there's no data exfiltration — something no proprietary vendor can offer. Our NemoClaw sandboxing adds 4-layer agent isolation that exceeds what proprietary tools provide."*

### "We need SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certification"
*"For self-hosted deployment, you're running the software on your own certified infrastructure. For managed deployment, we're working toward SOC 2. In the meantime, our immutable audit trails, encryption at rest (AES-256-GCM), RBAC, and DB-enforced audit logs exceed what most certified vendors actually implement."*

### "AI makes mistakes / we can't trust AI decisions"
*"Every alert requires human review. Every AI finding includes the complete reasoning chain — you can see exactly WHY it flagged something, verify every source citation, and override any decision. The human is always in the loop."*

### "This sounds expensive to run"
*"The Community edition is free forever to self-host. Professional starts at $2,500/month — that's less than the cost of a single SAR filing analyst. If we prevent even one insider incident ($6.9M average), the platform pays for itself 38 times over."*

### "We don't have GPU hardware"
*"You don't need it. Our cloud inference mode uses DeepSeek V3.2 — an open-source model via API. No GPU required. When you're ready for on-premises AI, add NemoClaw with an NVIDIA GPU."*
